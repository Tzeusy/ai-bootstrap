# Epic coordination

Use for a programme with several packages or shared prerequisites. The
[delivery model](../../../references/delivery-model.md) owns branch/session choice.

Keep the original finite accepted outcome and its required activation/runtime
proof visible in the existing epic. Child closure counts are scheduling hints,
not an outcome verdict. A foundation or dark merge leaves the larger outcome
partial. Amendment/deferral needs its governing authority and records the delta.

Allocate one persistent owner per bounded outcome. Select a coherent package from
existing beads, or author missing scope through beads-writer. Do not dispatch an
unbounded epic as one huge PR or split purely to meet a child-count limit. No
Team Lead/subworker mini-coordinator machinery is needed; specialists advise the
accountable owner. Disjoint outcomes may have separate owners/worktrees under
the existing capacity budget, without overlapping contract decisions.

A childless epic needs a current accepted packet, not automatic decomposition.
Hold its claim while authoring children sequentially; preserve correct parent
links and keep gates on dependent runnable tasks (Beads rejects task blockers
on epics). For programme-level contracts, settle adopted shared interfaces and
source feasibility once before dispatching dependent implementation.

When several programmes need the same substrate, select one existing canonical
prerequisite and owner, link dependents and finish the runnable prerequisite chain.
Do not implement separate runners/evaluators on several epic branches. Existing
ranker scope/prerequisite inheritance must keep that chain discoverable, even
when the prerequisite belongs outside the selected outcome.

An owner-held gate blocks its dependent work only. A CI/review wait is technical,
not a new owner decision. Routine correctness stays in the current package. A
new subsystem/trust/architecture boundary returns to its governing gate; create
one prerequisite only if a new ownership boundary is actually required. Preserve
published useful work and local recovery state through waits and preemption.
